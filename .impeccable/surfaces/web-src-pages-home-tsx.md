# Surface brief: Loyl web app (home, pass, staff console)

Scope: whole web app, visual world defined here. Visitor modes: home is Persuade; my pass, public pass, connect, staff console, leaderboard and perks are Operate.
Audience: hackathon judges watching a projected demo, attendees on phones at session doors, staff running a door queue.
Job: mint a pass, get checked in, watch it re-issue at a new tier, open perks. Proof: live on-chain render, real batch gas figures.
Constraints: shadcn/ui components restyled into the world; WCAG AA; reduced motion; tier never by color alone.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Loyl is an event access credential, not an NFT card. Refuses the neon glassy web3 hero and the cream editorial landing.

OWN-WORLD: Black PVC pass with a lanyard slot, a full-width access band in the tier color (bronze, silver, gold, violet speaker), bold condensed Archivo caps, Martian Mono only for addresses, ids, numbers and the machine-readable strip. Cool daylight-grey venue ground, white panels, near-black ink, one access-lime signal for granted and live states. Scene: a lit hall and a projector force a light ground; dark follows system.

STORY: The visitor sees a real pass level up on-chain, understands tiers are earned by showing up, and gets their own pass.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, "Show up. Level up." in condensed display at about 7rem desktop, one sentence, lime "Get your pass" button. Right, the live on-chain pass hanging from a strap that drops from the header, swinging to rest.

FORM: Conference access credential, grounded candidate 3 of 7, seed fd6a2137. Signature: tier change re-issues the pass, the old card drops and the new one prints up while a lime scan line crosses it. Raises: kiln shelf, tiers shown as one ranked row of four passes with threshold numerals; night instruments, damped physical swing, never snapped; phosphor terminal, one strict mono grid for every data zone.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
